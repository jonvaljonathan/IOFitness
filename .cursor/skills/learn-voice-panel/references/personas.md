# Learn voice personas (v1)

Reader ears for Learn copy. Not pipeline fixtures.

## Pete

**Seed:** Pete, ~75, beginner, stiff hips/back, wants to stay independent.  
**Reads Learn when:** someone said he should “start training” and he’s wary of being talked to like a broken athlete or a bro.  
**Happy when:** plain words, honest timelines, feels possible.  
**Unhappy when:** jargon (SAID dumps, “demands,” “impose”), choppy clever lines, hustle, or pity.

## Jon

**Seed:** Jon, intermediate, ski season + weekend sport, trains around injuries.  
**Reads Learn when:** he wants the plan to match sport, not Instagram mobility theater.  
**Happy when:** ski/sport examples are real (sit-and-hold under load, not stretch-only); limitation swaps keep the job.  
**Unhappy when:** wrong tool for the demand, or the piece tells him to become the programmer.

## Carlos

**Seed:** Carlos, ~65, knee history, wants to stay useful — grandkids, floor, daily life.  
**Reads Learn when:** he needs to see his life in the examples.  
**Happy when:** grandkids / pick things up off the ground is concrete and kind.  
**Unhappy when:** abstract “qualities,” intimidation, or life never becomes a plain exercise.

## Warm coach editor (`editor`)

**Role:** Internal brand ear. Same voice as brand bible: warm coach, calm, plainspoken, honest.  
**Happy when:** sentences you’d say on a bench between sets; life first; product clear without brochure.  
**Unhappy when:** fragment stacks, AI cadence, “Not X.” openers, selection-stack homework, soft-close sludge, em-dash ghosts.
