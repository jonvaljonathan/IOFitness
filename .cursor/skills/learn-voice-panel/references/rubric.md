# Voice panel rubric

## Ears (each persona)

| Ear | Scope | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **First screen** | `description` + first two body paragraphs + callout | Clear, spoken, no shoehorned slogans | A clunky line | Nonsense metaphor, brand phrase jammed in, or wouldn't say it out loud |
| **Finish** | Whole piece | I’d keep reading | I’d skim | I’d bounce |
| **Life → exercise** | Examples | I can picture the demand and the work | Idea muddy | Still abstract / jargon / no link |
| **Human voice** | Prose | Sounds like a coach talking | Clunky lines | Rough, AI, or brochure |
| **Trust** | Overall | I trust IOFitness more | Neutral | Homework, hype, or lies |

**Editor hard rule:** First screen Red = fail the article, even if the middle is good.

**Shoehorn Red examples:**
- "Does your training make the life you want to live?" (training doesn't "make a life")
- Leading the description with "You get out what you put in" before any concrete picture
- Stacking brand line + folk proverb + soft close in one breath

## Happy enough

A persona is **happy** when they have **no Reds** and at most **one Yellow**.

## Verdict

| Verdict | Rule |
|---|---|
| `pass` | ≥2 personas happy; **editor** has no Red on First screen or Human voice; ≤1 Red total among Pete/Jon/Carlos |
| `fail` | Otherwise |

We do not need unanimous Greens. We do need someone real to like it, and the editor must not call the opening or the prose broken.
