# Voice panel rubric

## Ears (each persona)

| Ear | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Finish** | I’d keep reading | I’d skim | I’d bounce |
| **Life → exercise** | I can picture the demand and the work | Idea muddy | Still abstract / jargon / no link |
| **Human voice** | Sounds like a coach talking | Clunky lines | Rough, AI, or brochure |
| **Trust** | I trust IOFitness more | Neutral | Homework, hype, or lies |

## Happy enough

A persona is **happy** when they have **no Reds** and at most **one Yellow**.

## Verdict

| Verdict | Rule |
|---|---|
| `pass` | ≥2 personas happy; **editor** has no Red on Human voice; ≤1 Red total among Pete/Jon/Carlos |
| `fail` | Otherwise |

We do not need unanimous Greens. We do need someone real to like it, and the editor must not call the prose broken.
