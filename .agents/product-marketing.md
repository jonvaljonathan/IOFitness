# IOFitness product marketing context

Short operational brief for copywriting, copy-editing, and CRO skills.
Full brand system: `docs/brand-bible.md`. Learn register: `.cursor/skills/learn-copy-tone/SKILL.md`.

## Product

IOFitness is adaptive training that stays with you — monitors what you did, adjusts the plan, and programs the next work. It is not a one-shot AI workout generator.

**Brand line:** Get better.

**Frame lines (rotate, don’t mash into slogans):**
1. No matter where you’re starting, you can get better. We’ll help you do it safely, progressively, and honestly.
2. You can get better from here. We’ll build the path with you: progressive, safe, and straight with you.
3. Getting better is possible from where you are. We stick with you and program it properly.

**Soft close:** `IOFitness is built to…` (active). Never `is being designed…`.

## Audience

Adults who will train when the plan is clear and honest. Not an age niche.  
Ignore: ultra-specialists; people who want comforting lies.

## Voice

Warm coach. Calm. Plainspoken. Celebrates real wins. Not demanding. Unapologetically truthful — including hard timelines.  
One voice on homepage, Learn, app, and ads.

## Claims

No diagnosis. No guaranteed results. No fake precision.  
Learn: citations at the bottom. Marketing: light evidence OK, not a research paper.

## Visual (for page direction)

Paper `#f3efe6`, field `#fffcf7`, ink `#1a1714`, muted `#5e574e`, forest accent `#2f4a3c`.  
Fraunces on the **IOFitness wordmark only**. Source Sans 3 for everything else.  
No photography for now. No AI glow. No hustle-bro red/black.

## Competitive wedge

Own the trainer that stays with you. Refuse to position as “ChatGPT, but workouts.”
